# Broadband Diagnostics UI Reference

Use ThreeUI as a mood and interaction reference, not as source content.

## Direction

- Keep the product domain first: MTN/Airtel routers, current 4G/4G+/5G connection, Wi-Fi settings, connected devices, signal quality, diagnostics, and history.
- Borrow only the feel: layered depth, frosted panels, refined controls, smooth motion, confident spacing, and high-quality status surfaces.
- Prefer broadband-native visuals: towers, radio layers, carrier aggregation, router, home clients, spectrum, latency, and signal history.
- Keep the first screen useful to casual users: plain-language verdict, current connection, 5G state, Wi-Fi state, connected devices, and one clear next action.
- Advanced mode may expose raw metrics, but default mode should translate them into everyday language.

## Guardrails

- Do not copy or embed literal ThreeUI demo pages, iframes, nature scenes, landing copy, card images, or bundled assets.
- Do not use unrelated nature, lifestyle, or landing-page content inside the broadband dashboard.
- Do not make the dashboard a marketing hero page; keep it an operational diagnostics tool.
- Do not hide required network facts behind decoration.
- Do not reintroduce copied demo iframes, unrelated card assets, or ThreeUI landing-page copy.

## Expected Panels

- Connection summary: current connection type, operator, 5G state, Wi-Fi, connected devices.
- Signal map: network to cellular layer to router to home devices.
- Diagnostics: weak signal, interference, likely congestion, and local-link issues.
- Derived metrics: total aggregated bandwidth, carrier aggregation count, band mix, MIMO rank, QAM, and WAN/SIM details.
- History: SINR, RSRP, MCS, and local latency trends.
